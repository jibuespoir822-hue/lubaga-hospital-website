WHO WE ARE

About Lubaga Hospital

Our history, vision and commitment to healthcare.

OUR HISTORY

A Long History of Service

Lubaga Hospital, also known as Uganda Martyrs Hospital Lubaga, was founded in 1899 by the Missionary Sisters of Our Lady of Africa.

The hospital is located in Kampala, Uganda. It is committed to providing sustainable, quality and compassionate healthcare services.

Our Vision

To be a state-of-the-art healthcare facility in Africa.

Our Mission

To provide sustainable quality and compassionate health services to the community for God's glory.

Our Core Values

  • Respect for human life and dignity.
  • Justice and equality.
  • Integrity and transparency.
  • Innovation and quality healthcare.
  • Accountability and commitment.
